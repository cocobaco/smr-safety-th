## One common first question

**Under a clearly defined loss of normal power and heat rejection, what evidence supports continued core cooling for each design?**

Use a common evidence worksheet, not an identical accident sequence imposed across designs:

1. Exact design/version, initial plant state and initiating event.
2. Credited heat-removal path and ultimate heat sink.
3. Required inventories, valves, power/instrumentation and operator actions.
4. Claimed duration, replenishment/access conditions and environmental limits.
5. Experimental/model validation and scaling uncertainty.
6. Multi-module/common-cause conditions where applicable.
7. What is documented, missing or restricted; effect on a proposed NRSRS study.

These are proposed investigation criteria. No calculations or findings of failure have been made.
