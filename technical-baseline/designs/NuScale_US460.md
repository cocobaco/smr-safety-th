# NuScale US460 — United States

Baseline prepared 30 October 2026. Initial evidence record; not a safety verdict or procurement recommendation. Facts, developer claims and proposed research questions are distinguished below. Source IDs resolve to ../evidence/sources.json.

**Identity:** the US460 reference is six 77 MWe modules (462 MWe arithmetic total); keep earlier US600 and other module versions separate [S04]. Module/plant output and net/gross conventions must remain explicit.

**Verified milestone:** NRC announced standard design approval on 29 May 2025 [S04]. This is not site-specific permission to construct or operate. No operating deployment was established in the inspected evidence. Direct NRC retrieval was blocked; indexed primary text was available.

**Safety features to examine:** passive physical processes and modules partly immersed in a below-ground safety-related pool [S04]. Review the actual US460 safety case before reusing older design conclusions.

**Proposed research question:** How do shared-pool conditions and simultaneous module demands affect extended heat removal, and which event combinations are included in the approved analysis?

**Evidence to obtain next:** US460 final safety evaluation, applicable extended-cooling/reactivity methodology, pool inventory/temperature boundary conditions, multi-module assumptions and validation basis.

**Interpretation boundary:** pool dependence is a proposed investigation topic, not a finding of inadequate safety. No cross-design core-damage-frequency ranking is attempted.
