Baseline prepared 30 October 2026. Initial evidence record; not a safety verdict or procurement recommendation. Facts, developer claims and proposed research questions are distinguished below. Source IDs resolve to ../evidence/sources.json.

**Identity:** natural-circulation direct-cycle BWR. Vendor UK material reports 300 MWe net [S06]. Darlington's 2026 protocol states a 327 MWe project scope without resolving electrical basis in this review [S05]. Retain both source-specific numbers until reconciled; do not silently replace one.

**Verified milestones:** Darlington Unit 1 is described as under construction; Canadian construction licence issued 4 April 2025; operating-licence application received 25 March 2026 [S05]. An application is not an operating licence. This record concerns Canada, even though the user groups the design under the USA.

**Safety features to examine:** natural circulation and isolation condensers; vendor descriptions condition longer cooling on additional pool water [S06]. Duration and replenishment claims need scenario-specific evidence.

**Proposed research question:** What governs isolation-condenser availability, isolation/actuation, pool heat rejection and water replenishment under loss of normal power/heat rejection?
