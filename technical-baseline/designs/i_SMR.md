# i-SMR — Republic of Korea

Baseline prepared 30 October 2026. Initial evidence record; not a safety verdict or procurement recommendation. Facts, developer claims and proposed research questions are distinguished below. Source IDs resolve to ../evidence/sources.json.

**Identity:** Korean innovative SMR; developer catalogue reports 170 MWe per module and 680 MWe for four modules [S07]. Do not substitute SMART or SMART100 licensing history.

**Verified milestone:** developer announcement reports submission of a standard-design-approval application to NSSC on 27 February 2026 [S08]. No approval was established here. This milestone is based on indexed Korean primary text; formal regulator acceptance/docket remains a follow-up.

**Developer features/claims to examine:** passive cooling, boron-free operation and integrated multi-module control [S07]. Brochure claims of indefinite cooling require their full boundary conditions and validation evidence.

**Proposed research question:** Which assumptions support long-term passive heat removal, and how are boron-free reactivity control and module interactions addressed across the defined accident sequences?

**Evidence to obtain next:** dated application design baseline; regulator review scope; transient/criticality methodology; passive-system test reports and scaling justification.

**Interpretation boundary:** development targets and brochure statements are not measured fleet results or demonstrated compliance.
