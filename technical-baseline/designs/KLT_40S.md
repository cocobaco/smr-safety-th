# KLT-40S / Akademik Lomonosov — Russia

Baseline prepared 30 October 2026. Initial evidence record; not a safety verdict or procurement recommendation. Facts, developer claims and proposed research questions are distinguished below. Source IDs resolve to ../evidence/sources.json.

**Identity:** floating compact-loop PWR installation. IAEA's 2025 table reports two units, each 35 MWe gross and 32 MW reference power [S09]. Record the floating plant separately from any land-based adaptation.

**Verified readiness evidence:** IAEA describes commercial operation since May 2020 [S01]; its March 2025 table lists both units operational [S09]. Rosenergoatom's 2024 fleet passage independently identifies two KLT-40S units [S10]. This is deployment evidence, not a live availability assessment.

**Selection rationale (interpretation):** choose KLT-40S as the Russian entry because demonstrated commercial electricity supply is the readiness criterion. This does not identify the best export option or prove readiness for a Thai land-based project. Keep RITM-200N on a future land-based watchlist; do not transfer icebreaker experience as proof of land-plant maturity.

**Proposed research question:** Which safety and operating lessons from the floating installation are transferable to another setting, and which depend on marine heat sinks, platform/site hazards, logistics and fuel-management arrangements?

**Evidence to obtain next:** public operating-experience reports, safety-system architecture, event/availability data, marine-hazard and long-term heat-removal analyses.

**Interpretation boundary:** the other four land-oriented profiles do not make a like-for-like siting comparison with this floating reference.
