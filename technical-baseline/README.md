# SMR design safety research

A small starting collection for NRSRS: ACP100, NuScale US460, BWRX-300, Korean i-SMR and Russian KLT-40S.

**Purpose:** choose evidence gaps worth investigating and develop defensible research questions. This baseline does not rank designs by safety or select a technology for Thailand.

## Working rules

Identify the exact version before comparing parameters. Separate verified facts, developer claims, interpretations and proposed investigations. Record source and review date for every consequential claim. Distinguish design review, national design approval, site construction permission and operation.

No institutional confidential records or partner documents are included. These are initial technical notes supporting the personal SMR Safety Thailand knowledge resource.

## First increment

Five short profiles, one comparison, one source register. No software installation or execution is required. Next work: collect the public safety-system evidence for the common heat-removal question and review it with a qualified technical colleague.
